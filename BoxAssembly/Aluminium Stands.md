Creating Aluminium Stands for the KoE Mirror Box
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
After assembling all components of the box and the origami structures you will notice that wires go underneath too which can get crushed meaning the box will not stand flat on it surface.
To solve this issue Aluminium feet were turned on the metal lathe. This is optional as a book of similar height placed on both sides can also ensure the box is level. Alternatively, 
wood/rubber blocks can be glued to the bottom surface. Only a brushed finish was achieved due to limited time but a mirror finish would go even better with the mirror acrylic.

For further improvements when creating the box a double walled approch would have ensured the KoE staying levelled when placed on the table regardless of the wires. Due to time constraints,
aluminium feet were turned to achieve a quality finish and aesthetic. 

The stock used for this project was 50mm but smaller stock would mean less waste material. 

Finished Dimensions for the Aluminium Feet - Quantity = 4 
  - Top - 20mm
  - Bottom - 35mm/ 40mm according to preference
  - Overall Height - 25mm
  - Taper - 15° degrees

Instructions 
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FACING OFF
____________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________
  1. Place the 50mm stock into chuck of the lathe and tighten securely uusing chuck key 
  2. Face off the clean end with a parting tool 
  3. Turn the main section to your preferred bottom diametre 35mm/40mm
____________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________
TURNING THE TAPER 15° DEGREES
____________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________
  5. Switch the lathe off completely.
  6. Locate the bolts holding the compound slide swivel base to the cross-slide.
  7. Loosen the swivel bolts slightly, just enough to allow the compound slide to rotate.
  8. Rotate the compound slide until its graduation reads approximately 15° relative to the lathe's longitudinal axis.
  9. Tighten the swivel bolts securely.
____________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________
IMPORTANT 
____________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________
The cutting tool itself must remain parallel to the lathe stock (the spindle axis), even when the compound slide/tool post is swivelled to 15°.
The tool cutting edge must be correctly aligned with the workpiece centre height.
The tool holder must be positioned so the cutting edge is correctly presented to the aluminium.
Do not confuse the compound slide angle with the cutting tool's orientation.
The compound slide is angled to generate the taper; the cutting tool must still be correctly aligned to the workpiece.
Always check tool clearance and alignment before starting the cut

  10. Cut the 15° taper and turn off the lathe. 
  11. Switch out the stock aluminium and turn the other 4 feet by repeating all above instructions 
  12. Alternatively, if the stock is long enough just torn the orientation and place the tapered edge back into the chuck 
  13. Once all four feet are turned use the parting tool to part of the feet at a height of 25mm 

NOTE: Height an final dimennsions can all be adjusted to individuals aesthetic preferences.
