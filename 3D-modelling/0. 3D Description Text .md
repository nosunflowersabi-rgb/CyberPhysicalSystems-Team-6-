Mockup production using Rhino 3D files and a bamboo printer


1. Disc structure for converting the rotary motion of a stepping motor into vertical movement
- Vertical movement: A stepper motor wound in a fishing line to move the paper structure up and down. 
As the initial movement was too slow, we increased the diameter of the circular component attached to the motor to pull in more line per revolution.

<img src="1.%20Disc.png" alt="Disc mechanism" width="300">


2. Structure that converts the rotation of a servo motor into reciprocating motion on both sides
- Clamshell movement: A servo motor, wire and 3D-printed parts allowed both sides to open and close together. The parts were designed to print without supports.

<img src="2.%20shell.png" alt="Clamshell mechanism" width="300">

3. Clamping structure utilizing the rotation of a servo motor
- Gripper movement: We tested a servo-driven gripper, but play in the joints prevented smooth movement, so we excluded it from the final outcome.
